rclone-drive

rclone-drive is a personal utility used by the site owner to synchronize the owner's own files with Google Drive using rclone.

Purpose

The application is not offered as a public service. It is used only by the site owner for personal file synchronization and backup workflows.

Google Drive access

Google Drive authorization is used only to access files and folders selected by the site owner for synchronization. OAuth credentials and tokens are handled locally by rclone on the owner's computer.

Data practices

This website does not provide user accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The application does not sell personal data or share Google Drive data with third parties for advertising or marketing.
