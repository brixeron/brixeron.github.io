Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal rclone-drive utility operated by the site owner.

Scope

rclone-drive is not a public application or commercial service. It is used only by the site owner to synchronize the owner's own files with Google Drive through rclone.

Google user data

The application may access Google Drive files and folders authorized by the site owner solely for file synchronization, backup, and related personal file-management operations initiated by the owner.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials and tokens used by rclone are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. File data is transferred between the owner's local computer and the owner's Google Drive according to the synchronization commands configured by the owner.

Sharing and sale of data

Google user data is not sold. The application does not share Google Drive data with third parties for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data-broker purposes.

Website data collection

This website does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Data retention and deletion

Local OAuth credentials can be removed by deleting or reconfiguring the corresponding rclone remote. Files stored in Google Drive remain under the site owner's Google account and can be deleted by the owner through Google Drive or the configured synchronization workflow.

Security

The owner is responsible for protecting local OAuth credentials, the local computer, and the associated Google account. Access can be revoked at any time through the Google Account security and permissions settings.
